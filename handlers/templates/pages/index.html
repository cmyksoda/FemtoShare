What is FemtoShare?

FemtoShare is a service for sharing images, videos, and other files.

On FemtoShare, only you can upload files. You can share links to those files with anyone, and they never have to sign up for an account.

FemtoShare puts you fully in control, which means:

  • You can share direct download links without forcing your guests to see ads or prompts to sign up for an account.
  • There are no restrictions on file type, size, or content.
  • Videos are converted to MP4 so they play in every browser, which also strips their metadata, such as location.
  • Every other file keeps its original filename, contents, and metadata.

FemtoShare is easy to self-host. See our source repo for details.

FemtoShare is a fork of PicoShare, which is written by Michael Lynch, and is licensed under the open-source AGPLv3 license.
